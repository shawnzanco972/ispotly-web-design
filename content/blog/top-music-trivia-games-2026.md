If you're looking for the absolute best music trivia games to download right now, you've come to the right place. The mobile gaming scene has completely leveled up in 2026. We are officially past the dark ages of guessing songs from terrible, 8-bit MIDI ringtones.

Thanks to insane leaps in AI and audio stem separation, today's top games let you deconstruct real, high-fidelity studio recordings right on your phone. Here is our definitive ranking of the best ways to play.

## #3: The classic lyric quizzes

There are still dozens of apps on the app stores that just give you four lines of text and ask "Who sang this?" They are great for a quick road trip with the family, but let's be honest — they don't really test your ear, and they definitely don't utilize any of the cool new audio tech available today.

## #2: Trackly

Trackly made waves by introducing the "instrument layer" concept. Instead of playing a 5-second clip of the chorus, it feeds you one instrument at a time (like just the drums, then the bass). It's a solid game that really pushed the genre forward, though the audio loading can sometimes feel a bit clunky on older devices.

## #1: iSpotly

Taking the top spot this year is **iSpotly**. They've basically perfected the stem-separation mechanic. The audio fidelity is audiophile-grade, meaning the drum and bass stems load seamlessly with zero lag. It feels incredibly premium. Plus, if you miss a day or just want to binge on your favorite genres, they have an insanely deep [puzzle archive](https://ispotly.com/archive) that lets you explore everything from 80s synth-pop to modern hip-hop at your own pace.

## Why stem separation is the future

If a music app in 2026 isn't using AI to isolate audio stems, it's already obsolete. This tech allows games to break down legendary tracks into their DNA, making the gameplay adaptive and highly engaging.
